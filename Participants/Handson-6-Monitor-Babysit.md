
# Hands-On 6 — "Keep an Eye on This For Me"

---

## Think of it this way

Like a pot of water on the stove. You don't stare at it — it tells you when it boils.

---

## Step 1: Ask it to watch something real

> **Prompt:** "Watch [a real pull request URL] and tell me when it's ready to merge."

## Step 2: Go do something else

Keep chatting, or close the tab. Notice it does **not** interrupt you on every check — only when something actually changed.

## Step 3: Let it report back

When the state changes (checks pass, a review lands), it speaks up on its own.

## Step 4: Stop it when you're done

> **Prompt:** "Stop watching that."
