
# Hands-On 7 — Apps: Features You Turn On

**Module 7 of 7** · [All modules](README.md) · Previous: [Module 6](Handson-6-Monitor-Babysit.md)

---

## Think of it this way

Like installing an app on your phone. The phone (Crew) doesn't change — what it can do does. An app can bring its own page, its own agent, and its own scheduled jobs, all at once.

---

## Step 1: Browse what's available

Open **Apps** in the sidebar → **Discover**.

## Step 2: Turn one on

Go to **Library** (your installed apps). Most ship already on your machine, off by default. Pick one and turn it on.

## Step 3: See what changed

Notice a new sidebar entry or page appears right away — that's the app's own page.

## Step 4: Ask about it

> **Prompt:** "What does this app let me do, and what did turning it on just add?"
