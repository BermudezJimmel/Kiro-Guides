## Before You Start

- [ ] You finished Installation and the app is open
- [ ] You see a chat box, ready to type in

**The one idea to keep in mind:** think of the AI as an associate-level team member. You give it a job description, a toolbox, and boundaries — just like onboarding a new hire.

---

## Step 1: See who you're signed in as

Open **Settings → Overview**. This shows which account is signed in and what it's running on. No terminal needed — you did the sign-in already on the "Set up Kiro" page during Installation.

## Step 2: Ask it, don't look it up

Type this in the chat box:

> **Prompt:** "What model am I using right now, and what plan is funding this?"

Read its answer out loud. This is the fastest way to see there's no separate "Crew login" — one account funds everything.

## Step 3: See the cost of asking vs. automating

Type this in the chat box:

> **Prompt:** "If I set up a scheduled job that just runs a shell command with no AI involved, does that cost me anything?"

**Good to know, for when it answers:** a job only spends anything when the AI model reads or writes something. A plain command (like checking disk space) costs $0. "Summarize today's tickets" needs the model, so that one costs.
